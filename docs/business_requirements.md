# Business Requirements

## Business Objective

Build an analytical data platform for NYC HVFHV trip data that enables
analysis of ride demand, operational performance, revenue, driver
economics, and shared-ride behavior.

## Business Questions

1. How many trips occur over time?
2. Which pickup and drop-off zones have the highest demand?
3. What are the busiest days and hours?
4. What is the average customer wait time?
5. What is the average trip duration?
6. How much passenger fare is generated?
7. How much is paid to drivers?
8. Which locations generate the most revenue?
9. What percentage of shared ride requests are successfully matched?
10. How do trip distance, fare, tips, and driver pay relate?

## Key KPIs

- Total Trips
- Total Trip Miles
- Average Trip Distance
- Average Trip Duration
- Average Customer Wait Time
- Total Passenger Fare
- Total Driver Pay
- Total Tips
- Revenue per Mile
- Driver Pay per Mile
- Shared Request Rate
- Shared Match Rate
